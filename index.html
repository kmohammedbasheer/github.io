Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Alif She Campus.

Students who have successfully passed their 10th Standard (SSLC / CBSE) and hold a Class 7 certificate from the SKIMV Board (Samastha Kerala Islam Matha Vidyabhyasa Board) or equivalent Islamic education are eligible to apply.
The Fadhila Islamic theology program is affiliated with and audited by the Council of Samastha Women's Colleges (CSWC), ensuring uniform high academic standards across Kerala.
The HSE is approved by the Govt. of Kerala and examination conducted by Kerala state HSE board.
The Degree examinations conducted bySNGO University
Safety is our highest priority. The campus features gated entry, CCTV surveillance in public perimeters, strict visitor logging, and emergency medical assistance.
